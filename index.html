BIO

Elara Vance is a rogue archivist from the floating city of Aethelgard, known for her uncanny ability to decipher lost languages etched into ancient star-charts. With a penchant for tinkering with clockwork prosthetics and a mysterious scar that glows faintly during solar eclipses, she spends her days scavenging forgotten data from the ruins of the Old World and her nights evading the shadowy Syndicate that wants her knowledge silenced. Her ultimate goal is to reconstruct the "Prime Frequency," a signal rumored to restore balance to the fractured skies, even if it means sacrificing the only home she’s ever known.


SKILLS

Elara Vance possesses a unique blend of scholarly and practical skills, making her a formidable force in a broken world. Her mastery of xenolinguistics allows her to decode dead languages instantly, while her expertise in clockwork engineering lets her repair and repurpose ancient machinery on the fly. She is an expert urban scavenger, capable of navigating hazardous ruins undetected, and a skilled tactician who uses her knowledge of star-charts to predict environmental shifts and enemy movements. Additionally, her ability to manipulate the mystical properties of her glowing scar grants her limited precognitive visions during high-stress situations, often giving her the split-second advantage needed to survive.
